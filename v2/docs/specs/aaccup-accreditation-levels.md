## 1. Level Classification & Benchmarks

Programs undergo evaluation to achieve progressive accreditation levels from the Accrediting Agency of Chartered Colleges and Universities in the Philippines (AACCUP):

| Level | Status Title | Minimum Area Score | Minimum Overall Mean | Standard Validity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Candidate** | Candidate Status | None | 2.50 | 2 Years |
| **Level I** | Accredited | 2.50 in Areas I–X | 3.00 | 3 Years |
| **Level II**| Re-accredited | 3.00 in Areas I–X | 3.50 | 5 Years |
| **Level III**| Qualifying Re-accredited | 3.50 in Areas I–IV | 4.00 | 5 Years |
| **Level IV** | Institutional Excellence | 4.00 in Areas I–IV | 4.50 | 5 Years |

---

## 2. Mandatory Core Areas for Level III & IV

To qualify for Level III and Level IV survey visits, programs must achieve benchmark ratings in the four core mandatory areas:
1. **Area I:** Vision, Mission, Goals, and Objectives (VMGO)
2. **Area II:** Faculty Credentials & Retention
3. **Area III:** Curriculum and Instruction
4. **Area V:** Research Culture & Publication Output
