# Task Force Delegation & Evidence Review Matrix

**Project:** IQArchive v2  
**Role Context:** Task Force Member & Program Chairperson  
**Governance:** Bicol University Internal Quality Assurance (IQA) Office  

---

## 1. Overview

During an AACCUP accreditation survey cycle, the College Dean and Program Chair designate faculty members to form the **Program Task Force**. Each Task Force Member is assigned primary stewardship over one or more of the 10 AACCUP survey areas.

---

## 2. Area Assignment Workflow

```
[Program Chair] ➔ Assigns Faculty to Area ➔ [Task Force Member] ➔ Gathers & Uploads Evidence ➔ [Dean Endorsement]
```

1. **Assignment:** Program Chair maps faculty members to specific AACCUP areas (e.g. Area III: Curriculum and Instruction).
2. **Evidence Collection:** The assigned member compiles required syllabus copies, faculty development records, and board exam statistics.
3. **Self-Survey Scoring:** The member assigns an internal preliminary score (1.00 to 5.00) based on compliance indicators.
4. **Submission for Endorsement:** Documents are submitted for Dean verification prior to IQA consolidation.

---

## 3. Review Checklist by Area Tier

| Tier | Scope | Typical Artifacts | Review Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Program)** | Specific degree program | Syllabi, curriculum maps, program enrollment, thesis titles | Per cycle (every 3–5 yrs) |
| **Tier 2 (College)** | College-wide resources | Faculty credentials, college research publications, minutes | Annual update |
| **Tier 3 (Institutional)**| University-wide | BOR resolutions, university charter, administrative manual | Central IQA managed |
