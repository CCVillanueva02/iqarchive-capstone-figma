## 1. Overview

Under the revised AACCUP survey instrument guidelines for State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), degree programs are evaluated across 10 distinct operational areas. Each area is assigned an official weight determining its contribution to the overall institutional mean score.

---

## 2. Area Weight Distribution Matrix

| Area Code | Area Title | Weight (%) | Key Parameters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Area I** | Vision, Mission, Goals, and Objectives (VMGO) | 5% | Statement, dissemination, congruence |
| **Area II** | Faculty | 15% | Academic rank, qualifications, recruitment, tenure |
| **Area III**| Curriculum and Instruction | 20% | Program of study, syllabi, instructional materials |
| **Area IV** | Support to Students | 10% | Guidance, placement, scholarships, student body |
| **Area V**  | Research | 10% | Budget, publications, patents, faculty research load |
| **Area VI** | Extension and Community Involvement | 10% | Community partnerships, livelihood training, MOAs |
| **Area VII**| Library | 10% | Holdings, subscriptions, digital catalog, physical space |
| **Area VIII**| Physical Plant and Facilities | 5% | Classrooms, offices, accessibility, hazard management |
| **Area IX** | Laboratories | 10% | Equipment ratio, safety gear, lab maintenance |
| **Area X**  | Administration | 5% | Leadership, financial management, records system |

**Total Cumulative Weight:** **100%**
