# Zone 2: AACCUP Survey Instrument Master Hierarchy

This zone models the official 10-area AACCUP evaluation structure from survey instruments down to granular benchmarks.

### Tables
1. [`instruments`](./instruments.md) — Master survey instruments (UG, Grad, Institutional).
2. [`instrument_areas`](./instrument_areas.md) — The 10 standard evaluation areas (Areas I to X).
3. [`instrument_parameters`](./instrument_parameters.md) — Sub-sections within an area (Parameters A, B, etc.).
4. [`instrument_criteria`](./instrument_criteria.md) — Individual evaluation benchmarks and requirements.
