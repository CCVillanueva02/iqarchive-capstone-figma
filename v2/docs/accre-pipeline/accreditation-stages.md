
**All 9 stage transitions are initiated and approved by IQA Staff.**

```
Stage 1 (Draft) 
    ↓ IQA initiates
Stage 2 (Preliminary Review)
    ↓ IQA initiates
Stage 3 (Evidence Collection)
    ↓ IQA initiates — but College Dean must first approve evidence uploads
      from their college before IQA can consolidate
Stage 4 (Evidence Consolidation)
    ↓ IQA initiates
Stage 5 (Feedback Integration)
    ↓ IQA initiates — Internal Accreditor reviews/comments during this stage
Stage 6 (Final Review)
    ↓ IQA initiates — Internal Accreditor comments still visible/addressable
Stage 7 (Revision)
    ↓ IQA initiates
Stage 8 (Submitted)
    ↓ IQA initiates — External Accreditor gains read access here
Stage 9 (Accredited)
```
---

## Document Upload Approval Chain

```
Task Force Member uploads evidence
    ↓
College Dean approves (college-level gate)
    ↓
IQA Staff reviews (final consolidation gate)
    ↓
[Optional, Stage 5-7] Internal Accreditor comments (non-blocking)
    ↓
Evidence is part of the accreditation package
```
