# IQArchive — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the IQArchive FAQ! This guide addresses common questions about using the Bicol University Internal Quality Assurance (IQA) Document Management and Monitoring System.

## 🔐 Access and Authentication

**Q: How do I log in to the IQArchive system?**
A: IQArchive uses Google Single Sign-On (SSO). Click the "Sign in with Google" button on the login page and use your official Bicol University (`@bicol-u.edu.ph`) email address. You do not need a separate password for this system.

**Q: I logged in, but I don't have access to my department's files. Why?**
A: Your account role and department scope must be provisioned by the IQA Admin. If you have the wrong access level or cannot see your college's dashboard, please contact the IQA Office to have your account permissions updated.

**Q: I am an external Accreditor. How do I access the system?**
A: External Accreditors are provided with temporary, restricted-access credentials by the IQA Office prior to the scheduled accreditation period. You will receive an email with an Accreditation Notice containing your secure login link and schedule.

## 📄 Document Submission and OCR Processing

**Q: What happens when I upload a document?**
A: Once you upload a document, IQArchive automatically processes it using Optical Character Recognition (OCR). This extracts the text to make the document searchable and attempts to auto-populate metadata. 

**Q: The system flagged my uploaded document as "Validation Needed". What does this mean?**
A: This means the automated OCR engine had low confidence in extracting text from your document (often due to low image quality, handwriting, or complex formatting). An IQA Member will manually review and validate the document metadata. No action is required on your part unless the document is returned.

**Q: What do the different document statuses mean?**
- **Pending Review:** The document has been uploaded and is waiting for verification by an IQA Member.
- **Approved:** The document has been verified, accepted, and is now part of the official compliance repository.
- **Revision Required:** The IQA Member has returned the document to you with feedback. You need to make changes and re-upload.
- **Rejected:** The document was denied and will not be used for compliance.

## 🔒 Document Access and Security

**Q: Can anyone view the documents I upload?**
A: No. IQArchive enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Only the submitting College/Department, the IQA Office, and assigned Task Force members or Accreditors can view your documents.

**Q: How do I access restricted files outside of my department?**
A: If you are part of a Task Force and need to view a restricted document, you must submit a **Document Access Request** through the system. The relevant Department Head or IQA Admin will review your request. Once approved, you will be granted a secure link to view the document.

---

## 📊 Compliance and Accreditation

**Q: How can I check if my department is fully compliant?**
A: Department Heads and Program Chairs can view their real-time compliance status by navigating to the **Monitoring & Compliance Dashboard**. This dashboard shows exactly which instruments are satisfied, which documents are approved, and highlights any overdue items.

**Q: How do I link an uploaded document to a specific accreditation requirement?**
A: Task Force members and Department Heads can navigate to the specific Accreditation Instrument. From there, select the target requirement and use the "Link Document" feature to attach an approved file from your department's repository to that compliance item.

**Q: How are we notified of an upcoming accreditation?**
A: The IQA Admin will trigger an official Accreditation Notice through the system. You will receive an automated alert containing the timeline, required preparations, and the specific Accreditor assignments.
